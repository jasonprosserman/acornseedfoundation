What they do

Children and families in Kingston-Galloway-Orton Park, Scarborough

The Reading Partnership works on the belief that parents are a child's first teachers. In Kingston-Galloway-Orton Park, it trains and supports parents to build reading into daily life, puts books into homes, and runs programs that make literacy a community project rather than a school-only one.

  • Parent-led early literacy programs
  • Reading resources and books for the home
  • Community events built around reading
Why we fund them

The Reading Partnership is community-designed and parent-led, and its results come from a neighbourhood that is often described by its challenges rather than its strengths. Our partnership supports a model that works because the community built it.
