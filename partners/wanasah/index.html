What they do

Black youth and their families in Regent Park and surrounding downtown Toronto neighbourhoods

Wanasah provides mental-health care for Black youth and families in Regent Park by clinicians who share their background. It was founded on the recognition that the mainstream system was not reaching Black youth in crisis, and that care has to be culturally responsive, close to home, and free of the barriers that keep families from asking for help.

  • Counselling and therapy by Black clinicians
  • Family and community support
  • Culturally responsive care for Black youth
Why we fund them

Namarig Ahmed grew up in Regent Park and had never seen mental-health services there. When she and co-founder Dr. Leslie Shanks asked every agency in the neighbourhood whether anyone would support Black youth, the answer was no, so they built Wanasah. Therapists who look like their clients, an "auntie" on staff, mothers brought in first. Three years in, they knew they had saved lives. They had turned down grants that would pull them off mission. We funded Wanasah so it would not have to.
