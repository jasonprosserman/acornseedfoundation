What they do

Black girls ages 8–16 across the Greater Toronto Area

Black E.S.T.E.E.M. (Entrepreneurs, Scientists, Technicians, Engineers, Economists, Mathematicians) runs hands-on programs where Black girls build, code, experiment, pitch, and lead. The organization started as a founder-led grassroots effort and has grown into a GTA-wide program with workshops, camps, and a network of mentors drawn from the fields the girls are being introduced to.

  • Hands-on STEM workshops and camps
  • Leadership and mentorship programs
  • Entrepreneurship training for teens
  • Exposure to Black women in science, technology, engineering, and business
Why we fund them

Black E.S.T.E.E.M. is Black-led, grassroots, and working at the point where confidence is built or lost: ages 8 to 16. The organization has grown steadily on a small budget, which told our advisory council the model works and that unrestricted, multi-year funding would let it plan beyond the next workshop.
